BodyMate modifications: extraction of 415 muscle meshes and 282 skeletal/related meshes; a 21-joint animation skeleton; continuous skin weights; inverse bind matrices; three authored motion clips (push-up, squat, curl). Source positions, normals and mesh topology are retained. Source system labels for fibularis, tibialis, subscapularis and levator scapulae are corrected for presentation. Iliotibial tracts use deformable connective-tissue binding. Laterality for binding is determined once per anatomical mesh from its spatial side to correct swapped source labels, including flexor pollicis brevis.

The configured genital-region exclusion policy is applied before extraction. Source identities and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `rigged-body.manifest.json`. This derived educational rig and its authored muscle-activation mappings are not measured biomechanics or muscle-force data.
