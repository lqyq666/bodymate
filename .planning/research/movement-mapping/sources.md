# Sources: evidence-backed movement mapping

## Production sources

- NCBI Bookshelf / StatPearls, [*Anatomy, Thorax, Scapula*](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK538319/) — its scapular movement section identifies upper trapezius and levator scapulae for scapular elevation. Used for `shoulder_girdle_elevation`.
- OpenStax, [*Anatomy and Physiology 2e*, 11.3 Axial Muscles of the Head, Neck, and Back](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/11-3-axial-muscles-of-the-head-neck-and-back) — Table 11.5 describes SCM’s opposite-side rotation/bilateral flexion and splenius capitis’ same-side rotation. Used for bilateral cervical flexion and left/right rotation coverage.

## Deliberate omissions

- Cervical lateral flexion is omitted from the first production catalog. The available sources establish that several muscles can contribute, but the frozen current-model subset does not justify the direction-specific mapping needed for a concise user-facing answer.
- No source is used for activation magnitude, EMG percentage, training effectiveness, diagnosis, rehabilitation, or exercise prescription because none is within Stage 5B scope.
