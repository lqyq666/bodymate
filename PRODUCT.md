# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People learning how a body area relates to movement and training. They need to choose a body area, inspect one structure in context, and ask a lightweight follow-up without losing their place.

## Product Purpose

BodyMate makes a focused anatomy-exploration workflow legible: full-body navigation on the left, a separate local inspection view in the middle, and one current task on the right. Success means a first-time user can identify the selected structure, isolate it, restore nearby context, and ask a question from a single input.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, encouraging. The interface should feel like a clean training lab rather than a clinical diagnostic product or a gamified fitness dashboard.

## Anti-references

Avoid medical claims, fake training metrics, crowded anatomy encyclopedias, and decorative dashboards with competing cards or duplicate chat inputs.

## Design Principles

1. Keep orientation and inspection separate.
2. Make the currently selected structure unmistakable.
3. Give each task one obvious place to happen.
4. Teach through honest placeholders, never implied medical certainty.
5. Keep interaction calm and accessible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve visible keyboard focus, named icon controls, readable Chinese UI copy, and a reduced-motion experience that retains selection feedback without pulsing.
